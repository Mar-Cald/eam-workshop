## A practical introduction to evidence accumulation models in visual perception research

This workshop is designed to provide the rationale for using evidence accumulation models 
in experiments involving perceptual judgments. These models offer a comprehensive approach 
by jointly considering participants’ choices and response times.

Evidence accumulation models can also handle multiple-choice scenarios (two or more), 
making them suitable for analyzing responses across a broad range of tasks.

The workshop will be held as part of [ECVP 2025](https://ecvp2025.uni-mainz.de/conference).
